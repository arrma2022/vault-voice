Privacy Policy for OfflineNotes

Last updated: August 23, 2026

OfflineNotes does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, audio, or text of any kind. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network connection — the app does not request internet access on your device.

Speech transcription happens entirely on your device using Android's built-in offline speech recognition. Your voice is never sent anywhere.

Transcripts you record are saved only as plain text files in the app's private storage on your own device. They are never uploaded, synced, or shared unless you personally choose to share a note using your device's share menu.

Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing to disclose to third parties, nothing stored on any server, and nothing to delete on request — deleting the app deletes everything.
